About Glow Theory Skin

Why the range is small, how products are chosen, and the promise we keep on every page.

Glow Theory Skin started with a bathroom shelf full of half used bottles and a simple question: why is skincare so hard to buy? Too many products, too many promises, and labels written like exam papers. So we built a small range on purpose. Fourteen products, each with one clear job, organised by concern instead of by marketing season. We write our labels and product pages the way we would explain them to a friend, including the honest limits of what a product can do. If your skin is happy with less, we are happy too. That is the theory, and it seems to be working.

How products earn their place

A product joins the range only if it does one job clearly, at a price we can explain, with ingredients we can describe in plain English. If a product cannot be explained simply, it does not get made. When two products would do the same job, we keep the gentler one.

The no miracle claims promise

Skincare can clean, hydrate, protect and slowly improve how skin looks and feels. It cannot rewind time, and we will never pretend it can. Every product page says plainly what the product does, who it suits and how long a bottle usually lasts, so you can decide with open eyes.
