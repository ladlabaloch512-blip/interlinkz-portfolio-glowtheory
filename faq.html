Frequently asked questions

Routine order, how long a bottle lasts, shipping and returns, sensitive skin advice, and the retinol and pregnancy note.

Morning: cleanser, serum, moisturiser, then SPF last. Night: cleanser, treatment or serum, then cream. The Glow Routine page shows the full order with the sets.

A 30 ml serum used once a day lasts about 6 to 8 weeks. A 150 ml cleanser lasts about 2 months. Each product page gives its own guide so there are no surprises.

Shipping is a flat $5 per order, and free on orders above $60. Orders placed before 2 pm usually leave the same working day.

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If a product arrives damaged or leaks, send us a photo within 2 days and we will replace it.

Start with the Sensitivity shelf: the Gentle Cream Cleanser, Daily Moisture Whip and Ceramide Barrier Repair Cream are the calm end of the range. Patch test any new product on a small area first.
