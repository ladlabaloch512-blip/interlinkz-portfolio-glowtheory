The Glow Routine

A simple morning and evening routine built from the range. Four steps in the morning, three or four at night, and nothing you need a spreadsheet to follow.

Morning

  1. Cleanse. Gentle Cream Cleanser, a quick lukewarm wash.
  2. Treat. Vitamin C Brightening Serum, 3 to 4 drops.
  3. Moisturise. Daily Moisture Whip or Ceramide Barrier Repair Cream.
  4. Protect. Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen, last, every day.

Evening

  1. Cleanse. Gentle Cream Cleanser, a slower wash than the morning one.
  2. Hydrate. Hyaluronic Acid Hydration Serum, on damp skin.
  3. Treat. Retinol Renewal Night Cream, two nights a week at first.
  4. Repair. On the other nights, finish with Ceramide Barrier Repair Cream.
